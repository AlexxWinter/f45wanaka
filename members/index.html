Good to know

Membership essentials.

Booking

Book right up to the door, or just walk in. Booking ahead helps with planning, but it's never required.

Pausing

Free with 7+ days notice, taken in full 7-day blocks, up to 12 weeks a year. Submitted via the pause form above.

No-shows

No cancellation fee, ever. If you can, cancel about 30 minutes before class so someone else can grab the spot.
