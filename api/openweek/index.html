Before you ask

The honest answers

Is this actually free, or is there a catch?

Three classes, completely free, no card details required. Joining the waitlist just means you're first to know when passes go live, it's not a booking or a commitment.

What if I can't make three sessions in the week?

Passes are valid for the full Open Invite week, use them on whichever three days actually suit you. There's no requirement to use all three back to back.

Will I be the only one who doesn't know what they're doing?

No. Every session is coached from the first minute, movements are demonstrated and scaled in the room. You won't be guessing, and neither is anyone else.

What happens after the three classes?

Nothing, unless you want it to. No auto sign-up, no follow-up call you didn't ask for. If you want to keep going, we'll talk membership then, not before.
