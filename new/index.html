What is F45?

Coached, functional, different every day.

A rotating program of functional workouts, cardio, strength and hybrid. No session repeats within a week, so you never plateau or get bored.

Screens guide you

Every movement is on the screens, and coaches are on the floor the whole time for technique and modifications.

Tailored to you

Go lighter and faster for fitness, or slower and heavier for strength. Same class, your pace.

A real community

You'll train alongside the same faces each week. That's the bit that keeps people coming back.

The week

A rhythm you can plan around.

The weekly split stays consistent, so you always know what you're walking into. Programming runs in 12-week series, refreshed in 4-week phases so you feel yourself progress.
